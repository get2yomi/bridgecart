Privacy Notice & Terms of Service

This notice explains what information NaijaBridge collects, how it is used, and the terms that apply when you use our purchasing and shipping service.

1. What information we collect

To provide our purchase-and-ship service, we collect:

  • Identity information: your name, and a government-issued ID document (such as a NIN, international passport, driver's license, or voter's card) and its ID number.
  • Contact information: email address, phone number, and username.
  • Address information: your registered street address, city, and state in Nigeria, and any per-order recipient/delivery details you provide.
  • Order and payment records: the items you ask us to purchase, product screenshots, quotes, payment references, and related correspondence.

We collect this information for identity verification, order fulfillment and delivery, customs and legal compliance, fraud prevention, and customer support.

2. How your information is used and stored

Your information is stored in our systems and used only for the purposes described above. Identification documents are used strictly for identity verification of shoppers before they are permitted to submit orders — they are not shared with third parties except where required by law or to satisfy customs/import requirements for your shipment.

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data, but no system can be guaranteed 100% secure.

  • NaijaBridge is not liable for product defects, product quality issues, counterfeit goods, or seller misconduct — these are the responsibility of the original seller.
  • Where a quality inspection is performed, NaijaBridge reports the item's condition as observed at receipt/inspection, but does not guarantee the item's fitness for any particular purpose.
  • The shopper is responsible for researching the item and confirming it is suitable before requesting a purchase.
  • To the extent permitted by law, NaijaBridge's total liability for any claim is limited to the shipping/purchasing service fee paid for the order in question — not the value of the underlying item.
  • Customs duties and fees shown in a quote are estimates only. The shopper is responsible for any additional government-imposed charges, including customs fees that must be paid before an item can be collected.

4. Returns and refunds

Returns and refunds are handled according to our stated policy: a return is only possible if the item is still with the original seller and the seller agrees to accept the return. NaijaBridge cannot guarantee a return or refund once an item has shipped or where the seller declines to accept it back.
